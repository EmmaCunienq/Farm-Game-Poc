# Visuels du POC

Ces images ont été créées avec l’outil intégré imagegen pour ce projet. La capture fournie sert de référence d’ambiance : illustration en papier, profondeur suggérée par les silhouettes et les ombres, sol organique. Les dessins sont originaux.

## Fichiers

- `village-atlas.png` : atlas RGBA transparent de neuf illustrations (maison, boutique, arbre, fermier, carottes, pousse, roseaux, fleurs, caisse).
- `clearing-ground.png` : sol peint de la clairière.
- `extra-crops.png` : atlas transparent des radis et des citrouilles.
- `home-paper-atlas.png` et `home-paper-sides.png` : mobilier illustré, armoire, coffre et détails de l'intérieur.
- `home-painted-surfaces.png` : trois papiers peints et trois sols peints.
- `farmer-paper-parts.png` : coiffures, hauts et bas peints du personnage personnalisable.

Les prompts de cette refonte sont conservés dans `PAPER_REDESIGN_PROMPTS.md`.

L’import Unity conserve l’alpha et désactive la compression. FarmArt découpe les sujets selon leurs bornes réelles ; les illustrations sont ancrées au pied et utilisées comme cartes verticales dans le monde 3D. Le shader URP utilise leur alpha pour découper les silhouettes et les ombres. Les sillons de terre, la mare et les panneaux de l’interface sont dessinés en code. Le personnage est découpé en tête, buste et jambes pour un rig natif simple.

## Prompt de l’atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D game sprite atlas, a square sheet arranged in exactly three columns and three rows of equally sized cells, transparent background. Reference: the user's village screenshot is a style reference only. Primary request: original cozy farming game sprites with the visual language of paper cutout illustrated village games: slightly oblique front view, chunky uneven dark brown ink outlines, watercolor/gouache washes, muted sage green and moss, warm clay red, parchment yellow, little hand-drawn details. Each subject is a separate isolated cutout with real alpha, fully within its cell with at least 10 percent empty margin, all centered in each equal cell. No divider lines. Row 1 left: small crooked timber cottage with steep russet shingle roof, cream plaster, a little round golden window and arched wooden doorway; row 1 center: charming seed shop stall with moss-green awning, hanging carrot icon sign with no writing, crates of vegetables, little lantern; row 1 right: a mature broad oak tree with textured lumpy sage foliage and dark twisted trunk. Row 2 left: a small original friendly farmer character facing camera, oversized round head, straw hat, teal overalls, terracotta scarf, tan skin, little boots; row 2 center: mature carrot crop with a few big bright leaves and three orange carrot tops visible above soil, no soil tile; row 2 right: a tiny three-leaf young sprout. Row 3 left: tall reeds and wispy wild grass clump; row 3 center: a rounded low leafy shrub with tiny cream and rust-colored flowers; row 3 right: a wooden crate of pumpkins with a little red mushroom beside it. Artwork should be consistent across cells and readable as small sprites. No ground planes, no cast shadows, no text, no logos, no watermark, no copies of characters from the reference. True transparent background throughout, no checkerboard painted into image. Output a square atlas.

## Prompt du sol

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: top-down 2D farming game terrain background plate, landscape 3:2 composition. Primary request: an empty playable forest village clearing rendered as hand-painted paper illustration with dark subtle ink flecks, watercolor gouache textures and the moody organic soil aesthetic of a cute paper-cutout farming village. Terrain only: broad central irregular dusty olive-earth clearing, patches of soft muted sage grass at edges, winding subtle beige footpaths leading from lower center toward the upper left and upper right, small scattered pebble marks and sparse tiny blades of grass. All shapes flat on the ground seen overhead, no perspective horizon. Keep the center and upper interior open and uncluttered for separately placed buildings, characters and crop plots. Rounded uneven silhouettes and lightly speckled aged-paper grain. Warm grey olive, faded sage, muted teal shadows, dusty ochre paths. Soft ambient daylight, gentle cozy mood. Entire canvas filled with terrain. No square grid, no tiled checker pattern, no buildings, no characters, no trees, no tall plants, no crop rows, no UI, no writing, no watermarks. Painted videogame ground plate, not a map diagram.

## Prompt des nouvelles cultures

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 2D game crop sprite atlas with exactly two separate isolated subjects side by side. Create original mature radish and pumpkin plants for a paper-cutout farming game. Match hand-painted gouache, muted sage/olive foliage, warm rich vegetable colors, thick imperfect dark brown ink contours, little watercolor paper grain. Left half: three small pink-and-cream round radish bulbs peeking above the soil with bushy lush green leaves, no soil patch. Right half: one plump terracotta orange pumpkin with curling moss-green vines and large leaves, no soil patch. Both illustrated front-facing with a slightly elevated oblique view so they read as upright cardboard sprites in a 3D world. Equal-size square cells, strong 15 percent transparent margin around each plant and large transparent gap between the two subjects. Keep all parts fully inside their own half. Real transparent background, no cast shadows, no ground, no text, no grid, no labels, no logos, no background checker pattern. Wide 2:1 composition.
